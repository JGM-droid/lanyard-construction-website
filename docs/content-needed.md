## Status Labels (Canonical)
- Confirmed: Approved requirement or decision captured in this planning cycle.
- Assumed: Working assumption for prototype planning; must be validated by client.
- Placeholder: Temporary copy, media, or detail used only for layout/prototype; not verified fact.
- Open Question: Unresolved item requiring client decision or evidence.

## Content Intake Rules
- Any unverified claim remains Placeholder until evidence is provided.
- Any inferred detail from existing website review is not considered verified.
- Project examples, testimonials, certifications, and statistics require explicit client confirmation before being marked Confirmed.

## 8. Evidence Checklist for Upgrading Placeholder to Confirmed
A content item can move from Placeholder to Confirmed only when all relevant evidence is present.

- Claim evidence: source document or approved written statement from client.
- Testimonial evidence: attribution details plus publication consent.
- Certification evidence: issuing body, credential ID, and validity status.
- Project evidence: publishable images plus approved project summary.
- Statistical evidence: metric definition, date range, and source ownership.
